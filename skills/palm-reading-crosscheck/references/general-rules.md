# General palmistry conventions

Use these only when the user hasn't given their own teacher's or course's rules. They summarise widely repeated conventions from Western and Indian palmistry. Schools disagree on many of them, so tell the user that this is a general reading, not any one school's.

Palmistry is a tradition, not evidence. Phrase everything as "in this tradition".

## Map of the palm (right hand, palm facing the viewer)

- **Mounts under the fingers:** Jupiter (index), Saturn (middle), Sun or Apollo (ring), Mercury (little).
- **Mars:** Inner (or lower) Mars sits between the thumb and index finger, above the start of the life line. Outer (or upper) Mars sits on the little-finger edge, between the heart and head lines.
- **Moon (Luna):** the lower palm on the little-finger side.
- **Venus:** the thumb pad.
- **Plain of Mars (palm centre):** called Rahu in some Indian schools. The wrist centre is called Ketu in some Indian schools.

## Major lines

**Life line** (curves around the thumb pad)
- Reflects vitality and major life changes, not length of life. Many schools say this outright, so never read it as lifespan.
- Deep and even: steady energy. Faint or chained stretches: lower energy or a difficult period.
- Breaks or islands: a disruption or a period of strain at that point.
- A branch toward the Moon: travel, or a move far from home.
- Common age scale: about 10 where it starts between the thumb and index finger, spaced roughly evenly down to about 80 at the wrist. Schools differ on this.

**Head line** (runs across the middle of the palm)
- Straight: practical and logical thinking. Sloping toward the Moon: imaginative and creative.
- A fork at the end ("writer's fork"): seeing several sides of a question.
- Short: quick, decisive thinking. Islands or breaks: periods of worry or changes in thinking.

**Heart line** (runs across the upper palm, from the little-finger edge)
- Ends under Jupiter: idealistic in love. Ends under Saturn: more self-focused or reserved.
- Forked: balanced between head and heart in love. Chained or broken: emotional ups and downs.
- When the head and heart lines run as one line (the simian line), the two are read together: intense focus, with feeling and thinking merged.

**Fate (Saturn) line** (rises up the centre toward the middle finger)
- Strong and straight: a clear direction in work or life. Absent: a self-directed or changeable path. This isn't a bad sign.
- Starts at the Moon: success helped by other people or the public. Starts inside the life line: family influence.
- Starts higher up the palm: direction found later in life. Breaks: changes of career or direction.
- Common age scale: about 20 at the base, about 35 where it crosses the head line, and about 50 at the heart line.

**Sun (Apollo) line** (rises toward the ring finger)
- Recognition, creativity or satisfaction. Several short lines can mean scattered efforts.

**Mercury (health or business) line** (rises toward the little finger)
- Read for communication and business sense. Many schools treat a missing Mercury line as a good sign.

## Minor marks

- **Marriage (relationship) lines:** short horizontal lines on the little-finger edge, between the heart line and the finger base. Clear and straight: a strong bond. Forked or drooping: distance or strain. Count and placement are read as timing, very roughly.
- **Girdle of Venus:** an arc above the heart line, read as sensitivity.
- **Ring of Solomon:** an arc around the base of the index finger, read as insight and an interest in people.
- **Intuition line:** a curve on the Moon side, read as intuition.
- **Travel lines:** horizontal lines on the Moon mount.

## Signs

- Usually read as **positive:** star (except on Saturn in many schools), triangle, trident, square (protective, often read as repairing a break), fish, flag.
- Usually read as **negative or cautionary:** island, cross or X (meaning varies by mount), grille or mesh, dot.
- **Moles:** some Indian schools treat a new mole as a sign tied to the mount it sits on. Always point the user to a dermatologist for any new or changing mole.

## Mounts (judged by fullness, best by touch)

- **Jupiter:** ambition and leadership. **Saturn:** seriousness and responsibility. **Sun:** creativity and recognition. **Mercury:** communication and business.
- **Mars:** courage (inner Mars) and persistence (outer Mars).
- **Moon:** imagination. **Venus:** warmth, affection and enjoyment of life.
- A well-formed mount strengthens its qualities. A flat mount weakens them. An overdeveloped mount is often read as having too much of them.
